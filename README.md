# Healthcare Patient Absenteeism & No-Show Analytics

## 1. Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Healthcare Patient Absenteeism & No-Show Analytics Dashboard designed to solve operational disruptions caused by missed clinical appointments. By analyzing historical scheduling data, geographic indicators, and communication channels, this solution provides clinic managers with the visibility needed to predict patient drop-offs, evaluate the effectiveness of text reminders, and optimize patient scheduling workflows.

## 2. Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI (Desktop & Service)
* **Data Engineering & ETL:** Power Query / SQL (Feature engineering on scheduling intervals, date parsing, and location mapping)
* **Analytics & Modeling:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for complex patient behavior metrics, non-attendance rates, and operational efficiency percentages
* **Visual Interface Elements:** Dynamic Gauges, Area Charts, Lead-Time Scatter Plots, and Geo-Demographic Bar Visuals

## 3. Key Keywords
* Patient Absenteeism Tracking
* Healthcare No-Show Prediction
* Predictive Operational Analytics
* Outreach Effectiveness (SMS Impact)
* Lead Time Impact Analysis
* Patient Demographics & Age Profiling
* Capacity Optimization
* Socio-Economic Welfare Analysis
* Cross-Functional Filter Panels

## 4. Core Metrics & Visual Components Explained
* **Operational Executive KPI Summary:** Aggregates a massive baseline of Total Scheduled Appointments at 71.84K, alongside a Historical Trend of Missed Visits at 0.95K, resulting in a tightly monitored Overall Attendance Efficiency Rate of 4.13%.
* **Impact of SMS Reminders on Attendance:** Quantifies the split showing that 66.31% (47.64K) of non-absenteeism interactions occurred without SMS flags (0), compared to 33.69% (24.2K) that engaged with text-based reminders (1).
* **Patient Absenteeism Trends by Age Profile:** Charts the exact count of no-shows distributed across a comprehensive lifecycle age spectrum ranging from 0 to over 100 years, exposing distinct concentration peaks around young adult cohorts (ages 20-30).
* **Waiting Time & Lead Days Impact Analysis:** Plots the direct relationship between scheduling lead times and the volume of no-shows, proving that immediate or low-lead appointments (0–5 days) have an incredibly high density of successful turnouts (a huge peak at 24K).
* **Geographical & Top Clinic Location Breakdowns:** Maps patient absenteeism counts simultaneously by neighborhood territories and specific medical branch locations, pinpointing high-risk operational centers (e.g., Jardim Camburi leading with over 5.1K entries).
* **Socio-Economic Welfare Distribution Gauge:** Integrates public demographic profiles tracking an aggregate index value of 8,853 patients under social support infrastructure segments to cross-reference financial aid status against hospital compliance.

## 5. Project Outcome & Impact
* **Minimized Operational Losses:** Enabled medical facilities to accurately capture historical missed-visit volumes, decreasing lost revenue windows stemming from unutilized provider time slots.
* **Optimized Scheduling Policies:** The lead-time metrics proved that extended wait horizons decrease appointment compliance, giving hospital networks a clear directive to transition toward short-window scheduling loops.
* **Targeted Outreach Programs:** Isolated specific age profiles and regional hubs suffering from high absenteeism, allowing clinic networks to switch from generic alerts to hyper-focused reminder paths.
